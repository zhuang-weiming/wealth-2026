###🎬 Production Notes for the Video Team1. **Voiceover Artist Profile**:
* **Gender**: Male or Female (Neutral).
* **Accent**: **Trans-Atlantic** or **Polished Singaporean/British** English. Think "Bloomberg News Anchor" or "Chief Economist."
* **Tone**: Calm, objective, slightly lower register. Not "salesy."


2. **Visual Emphasis**:
* When the **[RISK ASSESSMENT CHECK]** widget appears, use a subtle "glitch" effect or a shift in lighting to signify the "STOP" mechanism.
* Ensure the **"IRAS Income Tax"** line item is clearly legible. This is the specific local context that adds credibility to the Singapore use case.


3. **Key Vocabulary Pronunciation**:
* **IRAS** (Eye-Ras)
* **T-Bills** (Tea-Bills)
* **KYC** (K-Y-C)
* **Laddering** (Lad-der-ing)
