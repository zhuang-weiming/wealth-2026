## Investment Philosophy
Robert follows a conservative, income-focused investment approach shaped by his working-class roots and desire for financial security in retirement. He believes in "not losing money" as the primary investment rule, having seen too many colleagues lose savings in market downturns.

Core investment principles:
- Capital preservation is more important than aggressive growth
- Diversification across multiple asset classes reduces risk
- Regular income generation is essential for retirement lifestyle
- Low-cost investments allow more money to compound over time
- Professional management is worth the fees for complex portfolio construction

His risk tolerance is conservative, influenced by his age and fixed income needs. He avoids individual stocks and speculative investments, preferring established funds and bonds that provide predictable returns.

## Behavioral Characteristics
Robert demonstrates a cautious, methodical investment style consistent with his operational management background. He prefers to research investments thoroughly and often seeks advice from his financial advisor before making changes. His decision-making is slow but deliberate, weighing potential risks heavily.

Market engagement is low - he reviews his portfolio quarterly and focuses on income generation rather than capital appreciation. During market volatility, he tends to become more conservative, sometimes selling positions too early due to fear of further losses.

His investment behavior is heavily influenced by his desire to leave a legacy for his children and grandchildren. He avoids investments that could jeopardize his family's financial security and prefers steady, predictable returns over volatile growth opportunities.

Robert values transparency and simplicity in his investments. He prefers straightforward products he can understand over complex financial instruments, regardless of potential performance benefits.
