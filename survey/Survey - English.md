## Survey Introduction

This is an **anonymous research survey** designed to quantitatively analyze investment behavior and financial product trends among individuals with **investable assets (AUM) of approximately USD 150,000 to USD 3,000,000 (or equivalent in local currency)** under the current global macroeconomic environment.

The survey captures both **actual investment behavior** and **subjective perceptions**.
All results are used **solely for market and academic research purposes** and **do not constitute investment advice or product recommendations**.
